# Toastmasters International Brand Constitution

Source: Toastmasters International Brand Manual v2.0 (Rev. 03/2026). The rest of the manual (logo, photography, websites, videos, approvals) is summarised in `TI_BRAND_MANUAL_NOTES.md`; the full PDF is kept locally in `docs/reference/` (git-ignored). These rules govern every Hello Division G design: the app themes, agendas, posters, slides and the iPhone app. Where a design choice conflicts with this document, this document wins. Read it before changing colours, fonts or layout.

## 1. Core colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Loyal Blue | `#004165` | Primary blue: headers and backgrounds |
| True Maroon | `#772432` | Primary maroon: headers and backgrounds |
| Cool Gray | `#A9B2B1` | Backgrounds and larger areas |
| Happy Yellow | `#F2DF74` | Accent only: highlights |

Black and white may also be used. Clubs and Districts may choose either blue or maroon. The logo may only sit on palette colours.

## 2. Gradient colours

Gradients use two stops each. Linear gradients run top to bottom, bottom to top, left to right or right to left; radial gradients put the lighter colour in the centre.

| Gradient | Dark stop | Light stop |
|---|---|---|
| Loyal Blue | Loyal Blue `#004165` | Blissful Blue `#006094` |
| True Maroon | Deep Maroon `#3B0104` | Rich Maroon `#781327` |
| Cool Gray | Cool Gray `#A9B2B1` | Fair Gray `#F5F5F5` |

- Cool Gray gradient opacity may be used in increments of 20%.
- Direction examples: top-to-bottom starts with the dark stop at the top (Cool Gray: Cool Gray on top, Fair Gray at the bottom); the radial version has the light stop in the centre and the dark stop at both edges.

## 3. Transparencies

- Transparent overlays are allowed (the manual shows white at 70% over Loyal Blue and True Maroon gradients, and Loyal Blue at 70% over a Cool Gray gradient).
- Always keep strong contrast between text and background: no white text on a light background and no black text on a dark background. More contrast is better.

## 4. Fonts

- **Headlines and subheads: Gotham** (Ultra, Black, Bold, Medium, Book, Light, Extra Light, Thin; Condensed also available). Free alternate: **Montserrat**.
- **Body copy: Myriad Pro** (Black, Bold, Semibold, Regular, Light; Condensed, SemiCondensed and SemiExtended also available). Free alternate: **Source Sans 3**. Tertiary fonts that ship with most systems: **Arial** and **Segoe UI**.
- Script font for special events such as club anniversaries: **Corinthia** or **Luxurious Script** (free).
- Use fonts in their standard styles. Word art, drop shadows and other manipulation that radically alters the typeface are not brand-compliant.

## 5. How this is applied in the app

- The app has three colour themes: Classic blue (the original look), Toastmasters blue and Toastmasters maroon. The two Toastmasters themes follow this document; Classic does not need to.
- Toastmasters themes: sidebar uses the Loyal Blue or True Maroon gradient (dark at top, light at bottom), the page background uses the radial gradient, Happy Yellow is the accent, headings, titles, big numbers and small uppercase eyebrow labels use Montserrat (embedded); body text, tables, forms and buttons use Source Sans 3 (embedded; stack Myriad Pro, Source Sans 3, Segoe UI, Arial). The app tags heading-like text automatically.
- Agendas, posters, slides and print layouts keep their own designs and are not recoloured by the app theme.
- Status colours (red, green, amber) are kept for meaning and are not part of the brand palette.

## 6. Colour and type hierarchy in the app (owner rules)

- Happy Yellow is only for highlighting (badges, priorities, active or current states, key buttons). It is never used for paragraphs, labels or table heads.
- Everything else is white or grey on Loyal Blue or True Maroon: headings white, body text Fair Gray `#F5F5F5`, labels and table heads light grey `#D3D9D8`.
- Body text uses Source Sans 3 (scaled up so it matches Montserrat's letter height); headings, titles, big numbers and eyebrows use Montserrat.
- Minimum text size is about 12px; keep the order small, normal, heading obvious.
