# Toastmasters Brand Manual: working notes

Summary of the Toastmasters International Brand Manual, Version 2.0 (Rev. 03/2026, 41 pages). The full PDF is kept locally at `docs/reference/02330-001-0001-brand-manual.pdf` (git-ignored because it is Toastmasters' copyrighted material and this repository is public). Page numbers below refer to that PDF. The core colour, gradient, transparency and font rules are in `TI_BRAND_CONSTITUTION.md`; this file holds everything else that can bite a design later.

Contacts: brand@toastmasters.org (brand questions), trademarks@toastmasters.org (trademarks), toastmasters.org/TrademarkUseRequest (requests).

## Colour roles (p.13)
- Primary: Loyal Blue `#004165` (CMYK 100/43/12/56, Pantone 302), True Maroon `#772432` (CMYK 12/95/59/54, Pantone 188). Both may be used for headers and backgrounds.
- Cool Gray `#A9B2B1` (CMYK 23/7/12/18, Pantone 442). May be used for backgrounds and larger areas.
- Happy Yellow `#F2DF74` (CMYK 0/5/57/0, Pantone 127). Accent only: highlights and making elements stand out.
- Black and white may also be used.
- The old rule "maroon for Districts, blue for clubs" no longer applies (p.40): clubs and Districts may choose either colour. Headquarters may still use the colours to tag some of its own materials.

## Logo (pp.8-12, 18, 24)
- Clear space around the logo or wordmark is at least "X", the height of the wordmark. Minimum size: 72 px on web, 3/4 inch in print (Pathways logo: 72 px / 1 inch).
- Versions: full colour, grayscale, white (wordmark: full colour, black, white).
- Never: put it in perspective, scale it disproportionately, change element proportions, change any element or colour, overlap it with copy, slogans, symbols or images, put a patterned glow behind it, or place it on colours outside the brand palette.
- Clubs, Areas, Divisions, Districts and regions may not create their own logo, theme or tagline. Use the official logo, tagline and brand elements. Only a District Conference may have a custom theme.
- Removing the logo does not free a piece from the colour, font and photography rules.
- On photography: use only with good contrast, not covering faces, ideally top-left or bottom-right.
- The old emblem (the logo until 2011) is for historical context only. Do not use it in promotional material.
- Pathways badges refer only to the specific path or project they represent; never use them as decoration.

## Fonts (pp.20-21)
- Headlines and subheads: Gotham (free alternate Montserrat). Body copy: Myriad Pro (free alternate Source Sans 3; tertiary Arial or Segoe UI). Special-event script: Corinthia or Luxurious Script.
- No word art, drop shadows or other manipulation that radically alters the typeface.

## Photography and phrases (pp.23, 25)
- Photos should show engaged, empowered, supported people in Toastmasters settings (meetings, speeches, contests, training, conferences). Avoid landscapes, animals, children, food, medicine and architecture as the main subject.
- Cartoons, illustrations and clip art only as secondary elements. Always have permission for images used.
- Approved phrases (one per piece, with good contrast, not covering faces): Find Your Voice; Relax, present confidently.; Relax, speak confidently.; Communicate Confidently®; 100 Years of Confident Voices; Find your confidence; Become a better leader; Invest in a Brighter Future.

## Voice and tone (pp.4-6)
- Core values: Integrity, Respect, Service, Excellence. Brand promise: "Empowering individuals through personal and professional development."
- Voice: confident and compassionate, clear yet respectful, friendly yet professional. Warm, friendly, clear, respectful, universally understandable, professional, internationally friendly, succinct.

## Standards and procedures (pp.31-38)
- Needs a Trademark Use Request: non-paper items (trophies, ribbons, banners, certificates, clothing). Allowed without one: stationery, business cards, bulletins, newsletters, electronic media, websites, program covers, agendas, if directly related to the mission. Submit at least two weeks ahead; no rush requests; approvals expire and are not renewed automatically.
- Apparel: logo or wordmark on the front; back text at most 12 in wide by 17 in high; colours white, True Maroon, Loyal Blue, black, gray.
- Websites (p.34): follow Protocol 4.0 section 2. Do not re-host Toastmasters materials. Do not use membership-only or paid materials. **Add this disclaimer at the bottom of the website:** "The information on this website is for the sole use of Toastmasters' members, for Toastmasters business only. It is not to be used for solicitation and distribution of non-Toastmasters material or information."
- Videos: club name or District number below the logo; end credits with name, club, District, copyright symbol and year; written permission from everyone shown (guardian for minors); a disclaimer in the first frames saying the video is independent of Toastmasters International.
- Corporate clubs: never place the Toastmasters logo beside the company logo (opposite corners, or top and bottom); visuals follow Toastmasters branding, not the sponsor's.
- Gavel Clubs: same brand rules; use the Gavel Club logo wherever a chartered club would use the Toastmasters logo; no custom logo or tagline.
- District Conferences: a custom logo, theme or slogan is allowed for the conference only, must not infringe others' marks, and must be approved before distribution. Materials must include the District number and "annual conference". Sponsors go in a sponsor section, never next to the Toastmasters logo.
- Name badges, ribbons and pins may not be produced locally. Club banners only from a purchased digital design.

## Things to watch in Hello Division G
- Posters and agendas are Division materials: logo only on palette colours, correct clear space, no custom Division logo or tagline.
- If the website is ever shared beyond members, add the website disclaimer above to the footer.
- Do not host or re-upload Toastmasters-provided materials on the public GitHub Pages site.
- Contest and conference posters: a conference theme is allowed only for the District Conference.
